# Writing LinkedIn Posts Skill

## When to use this skill
- When the user wants to draft a post for LinkedIn.
- When the user provides a professional topic or industry news.
- When the user asks for a "viral" or "professional" hook for their network.

## Workflow
1. [ ] **Identify Topic**: Ask for the core message or topic of the post.
2. [ ] **Deep Research**: Use `search_web` to find the latest industry data, thought leadership trends, or trending discussions on the topic.
3. [ ] **Hook Crafting**: Design a "Stop the Scroll" first line (the hook).
4. [ ] **Body Structure**: Use a short, readable format (white space, bullet points).
5. [ ] **CTA Optimization**: Add a professional call to action at the end to drive comments or shares.
6. [ ] **Verification**: Ensure the post is punchy and within a reasonable length (typically under 3,000 characters, though shorter is preferred for engagement).

## Instructions
- **The Hook**: The first 2 lines are critical. They must create curiosity or offer immediate value.
- **Visual Formatting**: Use line breaks generously. LinkedIn users scan more than they read.
- **Engagement Strategy**: Ask a thought-provoking question at the end to boost the LinkedIn algorithm.
- **Tone**: Professional, authoritative, yet approachable. Avoid corporate jargon where possible.
- **Research**: Anchor the post with 1 clear insight or data point found during research.

## Examples

"Remote work isn't dying. It's evolving. 🏢

The companies that resist this change will lose their best people. It’s that simple. 

Are you team 'Hybrid' or 'Fully Remote'? Let’s hear your thoughts below! 👇

#FutureOfWork #RemoteWork #Leadership"
